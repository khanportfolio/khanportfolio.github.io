Strukturiert, detailorientiert und mit echter Software im Einsatz

I'm an Informatik & Management student currently working across software development and digital operations in Stuttgart, Germany. Rather than treating development as separate from the business side, I build the custom SaaS software my teams actually need — a QA platform, an AI content pipeline, a task/hours tracker, and a custom CRM — and then run the operational side too: sales pipeline data, SEO, and reporting. I also founded and grew Studio Abroad, an international study-guidance company, from a blank page to €8,000+ in revenue in its first year.
